# Foundations, methods, and experimental source ledger

Investigation date: 22 October 2026. This ledger complements the thermal,
transport, and information investigations. Metadata were retrieved directly
from arXiv's BibTeX endpoint for the explicitly selected identifiers in
`references/core.ids.json`; publication refinements are recorded separately.
Abstract/metadata checks establish identity and stated scope, not an independent
verification of all proofs. The textbook's elementary calculations are developed
explicitly rather than quoted from abstracts.

| Source | Role in the textbook | Check / interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Page, 1993 | Random bipartite entropy benchmark | Original abstract: full entropy formula was conjectured there; two-copy purity derived independently in chapter 1 |
| Popescu–Short–Winter, 2006 | Canonical typicality | arXiv and publisher metadata/abstract; typical vectors under a measure do not give a dynamical relaxation time |
| Bravyi–Hastings–Verstraete, 2006 | Locality, correlation and entropy generation bounds | Primary abstract and author-posted paper; hypotheses are finite-range/bounded-local systems, not every experimental Hamiltonian |
| Vidal, 2004 | TEBD construction | Original preprint metadata/abstract; chapter 31 develops the actual SVD/gate update |
| Schollwöck, 2011 | MPS reference and numerical methods | Original review and journal metadata; a review is cited as pedagogy, not as the origin of every tensor technique |
| Haegeman et al., 2016 | Projector-splitting TDVP | Primary abstract and published author manuscript; projected-flow conservation does not certify exact-state accuracy |
| Dziarmaga, 2005 | Quantum Ising critical ramp | Original paper inspected for Hamiltonian normalization and near-critical passage mechanism |
| Heyl–Polkovnikov–Kehrein, 2013 | Ising Loschmidt singularities | Primary abstract/metadata and accepted manuscript; finite-size zeros distinguished from robust thermodynamic cusps |
| Heyl, 2018 | DQPT review | Primary review abstract/metadata; used to map extensions and open interpretations |

| Kaufman et al., 2016 | Two-copy entropy and local thermalization | Original abstract and publication metadata; finite-system experimental evidence |
| Cheneau et al., 2012 | Correlation cones in optical lattices | Original abstract and author metadata; pair-correlation propagation differs from a state-independent commutator bound |

The complete selected-source index is generated from DOI/arXiv identities.
The original source bibliographies remain in version control, together with an
alias map. A publication year and an initial preprint year can differ without
indicating two distinct papers. The current edition does not claim exhaustive
