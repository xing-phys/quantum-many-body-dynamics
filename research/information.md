# Information dynamics evidence ledger

Investigation: 22 October 2026. Sources checked through arXiv abstract records and publisher pages; the bibliography records original titles and persistent links. A checked abstract establishes metadata and the authors' stated result, not an independent verification of a proof. Chapter derivations below are independently developed elementary calculations. Coverage is selected, not a complete index of 2026.

## Conceptual sequence

1. Entanglement: Schmidt spectrum → purity → quasiparticle production → ballistic growth → membrane variational principle → fluctuations and conservation-law caveats.
2. Scrambling: Heisenberg operator expansion → commutators → Pauli weight → butterfly front → thermal chaos bound and its hypotheses → verifiable teleportation.
3. Circuits: local gates → Haar second moments → swap identities and purity recursion → stabilizers → designs → projected ensembles and higher moments.
4. Open systems: dilation → Kraus channels → infinitesimal channels → GKSL → microscopic approximations → spectra and steady states.
5. Trajectories: recorded outcomes → jumps → diffusion → ensemble averaging → detector efficiency and unraveling-dependent entanglement.
6. Dissipative phases: dark states → uniqueness and mixing → nonnormal spectra → metastability → thermodynamic transitions → transport and reservoir engineering.
7. Measurements: conditional entanglement → hybrid circuits → minimum cuts and restricted percolation → replicas and Born weights → finite-size evidence and postselection.
8. Error correction: reference systems → erasure correction → decoupling → coherent information → dynamically generated codes → recovery costs and accessible certification.
